Privacy Policy for Nidham DailyDesk App

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and protect personal and business information when you use our mobile application.

Nidham DailyDesk is a business productivity and service management application designed to help organizations manage support tickets, tasks, customer requests, business communications, and daily operational activities.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality and security of your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • User Details: Name, email address, contact information, and username information.
  • Account Information: Login credentials, authentication information, user roles, and access permissions.
  • Organization Information: Company name, address, and contact details.
  • Customer and Contact Details: Names, addresses, and contact information.

2.2 Business and Operational Data

The App may collect and process business information entered or uploaded by users, including:

  • Ticket and Service Records: Purchased product details, reported issues, parts information, troubleshooting notes, technician observations, ticket status, and resolution details.
  • Task and Activity Information: Task assignments, work progress, deadlines, and activity records related to daily business operations.

2.3 Media Files and Attachments

Where supported by the App, users may upload files and supporting documents associated with support tickets, tasks, or service activities. These may include photographs, screenshots, PDFs, spreadsheets, and other relevant business documents.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

The App may process basic device and application information, such as device type, operating system version, and application version, where necessary to support app compatibility and functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core App Functionality

We use collected information to provide the features and services of Nidham DailyDesk, including:

  • User and Access Management: Manage user accounts, roles, and permissions to control access to application features and business information.
  • Product and Sales Records: Maintain product information, customer company details, and sales records for products sold by the organization.
  • Pre-Delivery and Warranty Management: Record product serial numbers, pre-delivery information, and warranty details associated with sold products.
  • Service Request Management: Create, track, update, and close service requests associated with previously sold products.
  • Repair and Inspection Tracking: Record inspection findings, repair details, service updates, and related technical information.
  • Warranty and Parts Claims: Manage warranty verification and parts claims submitted to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), including claim status and related updates.
  • OEM and Shipment Tracking: Track OEM reviews, approvals, shipment activities, and other updates related to service requests and repairs.
  • Service Resolution and Closure: Maintain service history, record resolution details, and track service requests through completion.
  • File Management: Allow users to upload and access supporting documents and attachments associated with product records and service requests, where supported by the App.

3.2 Communication and Notifications

We use contact information to send email notifications related to ticket assignments, status updates, service activities, and important service announcements. Users who subscribe to WhatsApp notifications may also receive relevant updates through WhatsApp messages.

4. File Access Permissions

Where supported, Nidham DailyDesk allows users to select and upload documents or images related to product records and service requests. Access to files is limited to the functionality provided by the App and the permissions granted by the user or operating system.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Authorized Users

Information is accessible to authorized users within an organization based on their assigned roles and permissions.

5.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share information with service providers that support the operation of Nidham DailyDesk, such as hosting, database, authentication, email, and WhatsApp notification services, as applicable.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, security, or safety of our company, users, or others.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Nidham DailyDesk, including:

  • Encryption of data during transmission and storage.
  • Secure authentication and access controls based on user roles and permissions.
  • Measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of information.

While we take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Nidham DailyDesk, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Users may contact their organization administrator or our support team to request account deletion or assistance with personal information. Deleting a user account does not necessarily delete business records, such as tickets and service history, that the organization needs to retain.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct or update your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable.
  • Request a copy of your personal information in a portable format.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain data processing activities.

These rights are subject to applicable laws and legitimate business record retention requirements.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details provided in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside of our primary operating jurisdiction, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the App.
  • Sending a notification through the App.
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
